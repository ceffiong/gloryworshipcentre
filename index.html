About Our Church

Building a Community of Faith

Glory Worship Centre Outreach is a charismatic and pentecostal church dedicated to reaching the world through the preaching of the gospel accompanied by the Holy Spirit.

Established on March 17, 2019 in Innsbruck, Austria, we have grown to include a branch in Tema, Ghana. Under the leadership of Senior Pastor Rev David Danso and his wife Mrs Deborah Danso, our church family continues to reflect the light of the Lord Jesus Christ.

Our Mission

To raise up Disciples of the Lord Jesus who are empowered by the Holy Spirit to advance the Kingdom through the Gospel of Repentance, Baptism, and receiving the Holy Spirit.
